Mahayan & Maelcore

A high-energy collision of psychedelic trance, industrial techno, and experimental bass — where happy bounce meets dark ritual.

About

Mahayan & Maelcore are a Europe-based DJ duo blending the ecstatic and the extreme. Playing together for over a decade, they weave psychedelic trance, industrial techno, and experimental bass into immersive journeys that move fluidly between joyful bounce and deep, ritualistic darkness.

Rooted in psychedelic culture and shaped by their multidisciplinary practices — tattoos, generative code art, visual installations, and sound design — their sets are highly curated and intentional. Years of deep digging and long-term music collection allow them to craft hypnotic flow, explosive peaks, and unexpected turns for dancefloors of all sizes.
